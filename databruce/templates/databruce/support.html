How to Support Databruce
This page was last updated on October 16, 2026.

Ways to Support

  • BuyMeACoffee (one time/monthly donation)
  • Redbubble (merch for sale)

If you've appreciated what I've done (whether with Databruce, Radio Nowhere, or my other projects) and would like to see them continue, you an toss me a few bucks at the link above. You can donate one-time, or join via the monthly membership option.

Additionally, I put a few items up on my Redbubble store. Currently just stickers and pins with the site logo. If you wish to support and also like a Databruce sticker, the option is there. Might expand this in the future if the demand is there.

While any contributions are greatly appreciated, donating is NOT required and is totally optional. I don't have any plans to paywall or restrict Databruce (or my other projects.) Contributions help support me as well as Databruce itself.

Why?

The decision to do this was one I went back and forth on for a while. I've never liked asking people for money. Without getting too deep, I wasn't really sure if my projects were "enough" to justify it. However, despite my reservations, I figured I'd give it a shot.

One of the bigger motivators behind my decision was wanting to eventually upgrade the site, which is currently on a VPS nearly at it's limit. Upgrading will give me a bit more headroom, and (hopefully) improve the site's performance.

The upgrade would also enable me to do things like host images (ticket stubs, show photos, newspaper scans, bootleg art, etc.), as well as self-hosting the database and get away from Supabase (which is limiting).

What Will The Money Go Towards?

Contributions will primarily go towards covering the site hosting costs, as well as future site upgrades like those mentioned above. In addition to helping support the site, you'd also be supporting a small creator and his work.

I've never done anything like this before, so if anyone has any ideas please let me know.
